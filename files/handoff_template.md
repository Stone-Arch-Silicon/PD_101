# Physical-design handoff

## Goal and acceptance criteria
State the block, interface, required clock, and checks required for acceptance.

## Reproduce
Source commit, environment activation, flow/tool/PDK revisions, exact commands, configuration, expected output locations.

## Results
Link simulation, experiment matrix, timing/corners, DRC, LVS, antenna, final artifacts, and three annotated screenshots from your own run.

## Decision
Selected candidate, rejected alternatives, remaining failures, unrun checks, exclusions, and next action.

## Peer review
Reviewer, reproduced run, discrepancies, and their disposition. Do not add a person's name without their agreement.
